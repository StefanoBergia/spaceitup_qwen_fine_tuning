What the Prompt Hands Over

The three prompts

Same task, same answer format. What changes is which coordinates the model is told before it draws. The highlighted spans are what the prompt fills in from the ground truth.

Base model and fine-tuned model, arm by arm

Waypoint error is the mean distance between the predicted and true route after resampling both to 10 points, in normalized image units (1.0 = the frame width). Errors are over parsed frames; the parse rate sits beside them. Beats to_goal is the share of frames where the model does better than a straight line from that frame's own start to its own goal, the strongest image-blind answer once the goal is known.

Where the error lives

A cumulative view of every frame's error. A curve further left is better everywhere, not just on average; the dashed line is the straight line to the given goal, the bar a blind predictor clears once it is handed the goal.
